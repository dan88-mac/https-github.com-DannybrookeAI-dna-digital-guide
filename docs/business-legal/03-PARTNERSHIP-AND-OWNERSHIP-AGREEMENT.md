## 5. Compensation & distributions

5.1 **Pre-revenue:** No salary unless both Parties agree in writing.

5.2 **Distributions:** Net Profit distributed **50/50** unless reinvestment is unanimously approved.

5.3 **Reinvestment:** Up to ___% of Net Profit may be retained for growth with unanimous approval.

---

## 6. Decision-making

6.1 **Day-to-day operations** may be executed by either Party within approved budget (≤ £/$ ______ per month per vendor).

6.2 **Major Decisions** require **unanimous consent**:

- Sale, merger, or dissolution of the Company  
- New equity, debt > ______, or change to cap table  
- Exclusive license or sale of core IP  
- Annual budget and pricing strategy changes  
- Related-party transactions  
- Litigation settlement > ______  
- Change of governing law or headquarters jurisdiction  

6.3 **Deadlock:** If unanimous consent cannot be reached within 30 days, Parties shall: (1) mediate with agreed mediator; (2) if unresolved, follow buy-sell procedure Section 8.

---

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 **Assignment.** Each Party assigns to the Company all IP created for Resync AI from the Effective Date forward.

7.2 **Pre-existing IP.** Listed in Schedule B; licensed to Company under perpetual, royalty-free terms unless excluded.

7.3 **Open source.** Contributions must comply with Company OSS policy ( counsel + CTO approval for copyleft in core).

7.4 **Domains & accounts.** Registered in Company name; shared credential vault with MFA.

---

## 8. Exit, buy-sell & departure

8.1 **Voluntary exit:** Departing Party offers shares to remaining Party at fair value (formula: ☐ 3× ARR × ownership ☐ independent valuation).

8.2 **Right of first refusal** on any third-party offer to purchase a Party’s interest.

8.3 **Bad leaver / good leaver** definitions to be finalized with counsel.
