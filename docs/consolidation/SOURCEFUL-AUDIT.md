# Sourceful audit — Why own a Resync-class SaaS

## Why someone wants this website

Workflow automation is a proven paid category. Buyers already spend on Zapier, Make, n8n Cloud, Workato, and Power Automate because brittle scripts and manual ops destroy time. A Resync-class product sells **reliability of multimodal automations**: vision/voice/text chains that self-heal, scoreable graphs, community templates, and a marketplace—with an internal agent fleet that keeps the SaaS sharp.

## Why people visit

- Search jobs-to-be-done (“moderate image uploads”, “heal webhook failures”)
- Template SEO and community showcases
- Founder demos / Desktop pack / iPhone zip for first look
- Comparison shopping against iPaaS leaders

## Why they subscribe

1. A workflow becomes operationally critical  
2. Credits or node limits bind on Community  
3. They need export, marketplace sell, or 50-module scale (Pro)  
4. Team wants audit, roles, SLA (Enterprise)

## Healthy benchmarks to chase

Gross margin 75–85% · NRR 110%+ · CAC payback <14 mo on Pro · Logo churn <8% · Self-heal success ≥90% on paid.

## Mapping to Resync surfaces

Landing acquires → Studio/Multimodal activate → Builder retains → Pricing/Stripe monetizes → Community/Marketplace compound → Admin agents operate the business system.

## Risks

Connector parity myths, unpaid COGS from model calls, legal drafts unsigned, Vercel Root Directory mistakes (“Index of /”), secrets in git. Mitigations are in the consolidation plan: gates, env-only admin bootstrap, scoreable quality, prune duplicates.
