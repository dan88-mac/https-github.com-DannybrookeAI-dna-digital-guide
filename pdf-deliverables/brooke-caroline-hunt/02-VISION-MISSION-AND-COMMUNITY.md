# Vision, mission & community — Resync AI

## Vision (10-year north star)

**Resync AI becomes the default “self-healing layer” for workflow automation**—the place teams return when integrations fail, launches accelerate, and communities depend on reliable software.

## Mission

Enable purpose-driven builders and organizations to **design, run, and export workflows that recover in production** without heroic on-call work—while growing a **template and community ecosystem** that compounds value over time.

## Brand promise

| Audience | Promise |
|----------|---------|
| **Builders** | Visual canvas → production Next.js export → measurable heal rate |
| **Operators** | Telemetry, quotas, audit trail, Stripe-backed tiers |
| **Community** | Reusable templates, spotlight, shared improvement—not one-off projects |

## Why users return (retention design)

1. **Workflow library** — versioned graphs and templates tied to their org  
2. **Credits & billing** — ongoing relationship via Stripe subscription tiers  
3. **Community gallery** — social proof and faster starts each sprint  
4. **Export ownership** — code they keep; Resync stays the control plane  
5. **PWA / mobile** — drafts and queue sync when they’re back online  

## Values (co-founder alignment)

- **Reliability over hype** — ship healing that works in logs, not demos only  
- **Shared ownership** — Daniel and Brooke at 50/50; decisions transparent  
- **Open community, proprietary core** — templates public; runtime & brand protected  
- **Long horizon** — 10-year roadmap funded by unit economics, not feature churn  

## Success metrics (year 1–3)

| Metric | Target direction |
|--------|------------------|
| Monthly active builders | Growth ≥ 15% MoM after PMF |
| Self-heal success rate | ≥ 90% on paid tiers |
| Template re-use rate | ≥ 40% of new workflows from templates |
| Net revenue retention | ≥ 110% on Pro+ |
| Community waitlist → activated | ≥ 25% within 60 days |
